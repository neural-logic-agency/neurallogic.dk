    Built to be trusted.

    Seven commitments that stand in every contract.

    • DecisionsPeopleThe colleague lays out the facts. A person weighs them and acts.
    • Writing to your systemsOffSwitched on only by invitation: where you ask for it, and where the rules allow it.
    • Statements about peopleNeverFiles on things: buildings, products, contracts, cases. No scores, no rankings, no predictions about people.
    • ProcessingEuropeUnder a data-processing agreement. Nothing is read before you have said so in writing.
    • Every quotationCheckedCompared word for word with its source before it reaches you. If it does not match, it is held back and you are told.
    • The archiveYoursConversion turns your files into a checked, addressable archive that works with any language model. If we part ways, you keep it all.
    • The first phaseStands aloneIt turns your files into the checked, machine-readable archive that any AI you use in the coming years will need anyway. Small contract, real cases of yours, and you can stop after any phase and keep what was built.
